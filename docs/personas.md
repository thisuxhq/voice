# User personas

## Persona 1 — Startup founders

Building AI products; need a voice surface quickly without assembling a full real-time stack.

## Persona 2 — Voice assistant developers

Shipping assistants; care about low latency, tools, and provider flexibility.

## Persona 3 — Customer support companies

Contact-center style agents; care about reliability, interruptions, logging, and telephony (Twilio / SIP in Phase 2).

## Persona 4 — AI companion builders

Long-running sessions, memory, and natural barge-in matter more than enterprise telephony.
